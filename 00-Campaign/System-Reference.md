# System Reference

Core mechanics as used at this table. Zone/Session/Encounter docs link here instead of restating formulas.

## Degrees of Success

| Degree | Trigger | Default Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Amazing Success | Exceeds Difficulty substantially, or nat 20 | Max outcome + bonus perk/boon |
| Standard Success | Meets/exceeds Difficulty | Baseline intended effect |
| Near Miss Fail | Misses by a slim margin | Fails, no active penalty |
| Standard Fail | Misses by a normal margin | Fails + minor setback |
| Major Fail | Misses by a wide margin | Fails + severe consequence |
| Critical Fail | Natural 1 (or expanded range) | Worst-case disaster |

### Default Consequences by Type

| Type | Amazing | Near Miss | Standard Fail | Major Fail | Critical Fail |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Combat | Bonus damage/effect | No extra penalty | Normal miss | +F bonus damage taken | Double damage taken |
| Social | Bonus Popularity/boon | No change in standing | -1 Popularity | Major Popularity loss | Alienates key host/faction |
| Skill | Unique boon | No change | Minor setback | Lingering Debuff | Severe/lasting consequence |

### When to Use the Full Ladder

*(GM heuristic, not official rules — keeps resolution effort proportional to stakes)*

| Stakes | Recommended Resolution |
|---|---|
| Trivial/routine, no interesting failure texture | Flat pass/fail |
| Standard significance, some texture wanted | 3-tier (Success/Fail/Crit Fail) |
| Signature, climactic, or audience-facing | Full 6-tier ladder |

Every Encounter block sets `Resolution Detail: flat / 3-tier / full-ladder` explicitly — not improvised mid-session.
