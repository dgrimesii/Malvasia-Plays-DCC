# Session 1 — Prep

## Plan: Scene 1 — The Collapse to the Threshold

### Beat 1: Surface Perspective Recaps
* **The Cold Start:** Cut immediately to the aftermath of the ground-shattering planetary shudder. Remind the table that every artificial roof, ceiling, and structure on Earth just pancaked into rubble simultaneously.
* **Player Spotlight:** Go around the table and have each player describe their split-second memory of the collapse:
  * *Sam (Mitch):* Under his project drift car turning wrenches in the garage; coveralls, steel toes, gripping the 4-way tire iron.
  * *Amy (Hilda):* Walking across the circus lot between the big top and living quarters; sequined performance leotard, sheer tights, ballet flats, bow in hand.
  * *Julia (Hannah):* Out back of her newly leased restaurant handling late-night launch tasks; chef coat, non-slip clogs, apron, clutching her 8-inch chef knife.
* **The Meeting:** Establish whether they recognized each other from the neighborhood or collided in the blinding cloud of concrete dust as total strangers scrambling toward the only opening in sight.

### Beat 2: The Descending Descent
* **The Broadcast:** The sky fills with the droning Syndicate broadcast announcing the opening of the subterranean facilities.
* **Sensory Push:** The surface temperature plummets rapidly; neon arrows and glowing industrial signs point down into an exposed concrete stairwell leading into the earth.
* **The Portal Slams:** Once all three enter the stairwell, massive blast doors grind shut behind them, cutting off the surface forever.

### Beat 3: The System AI Intake
* **HUD Initialization:** Retinal displays boot up, mapping their Health Bars (10 slots), Evade, and empty equipment slots.

> **READ ALOUD — Showrunner Announcements:**
> *Mitch:* "Mitch Vanderzee. Or should I say 'Z'? Look out, we've got a street racer! Tell me, does 'Z' stand for zero survival chance, or the sound your blown-out meniscus makes when you pivot? Nice coveralls, by the way. Very blue-collar."
>
> *Hilda:* "Hilda. Just Hilda. No surname? What, did your deadbeat dad run off with the vowels, too? You brought sequined polyester and ballet flats to a planetary slaughterhouse. The broadcast engineers thank you for the leg line, darling."
>
> *Hannah:* "Hannah Solo. Seriously? You stared down an alien registration engine and punched in a knock-off sci-fi handle while clutching a kitchen knife? Watch those clogs, Chef. Once the sewer water hits those socks, the smell never comes out."
